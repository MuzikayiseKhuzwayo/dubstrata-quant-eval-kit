1. *What specific market signal or evidence triggered this revision?*
2. *Was the inference driven by real-time order flow, prediction market consensus, causal knowledge diffusion, or macroeconomic liquidity?*
3. *Can we cryptographically prove that the model did not suffer from lookahead bias or post-hoc data leakage?*

## 3. Four Invariant Architectural Principles

Analyzing signal traces across hundreds of volatile market regimes reveals four fundamental principles governing Dubstrata's decision engine. These principles apply to **all** asset classes, market shocks, and forecast horizons.

---

### Principle 1: Decoupling of "Context Provenance" from "Streaming Inference State"

#### The Principle 1 Mechanism

In Phase 1 of a signal trace, the system displays the most recent ingested textual or macro document anchor within the 512-step lookback window. When two consecutive forecasts display the identical document anchor, users frequently ask: *"If the signal was identical, why did the forecast or weights change?"*

The answer is that **a narrative document is only one historical anchor; it is not the state vector**. The model's active inference input is a live $16 \times 512$ matrix comprising:

- High-frequency order book microstructure (bid-ask imbalance, order flow toxicity, realized volatility).
- Prediction market order book states (implied probabilities, entropy conviction, wash-trading haircuts).
- Causal graph belief diffusion vectors (Friedkin-Johnsen consensus shifts).
- Macroeconomic cross-asset solvency metrics.

#### The Principle 1 Learning

**The model does not suffer from textual inertia.** Even if zero new headlines or regulatory filings occur over a 4-hour window, the engine continuously re-evaluates risk as live microstructure, liquidity, and decentralized order books evolve.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Diagnostic Rule:** Never treat Phase 1 as a freshness gauge for the market state. Always inspect the **Phase 3 channel cutoff values** and **channel deltas ($\Delta$)** to determine what physical dynamics changed between ticks.

#### The Principle 2 Learning

**The model possesses an automated structural defense mechanism.** In calm environments, it behaves as an agile execution and trend model. Under stress or cross-asset contradiction, it transforms into a macro-aware causal model, widening its field of view across the causal graph to prevent being trapped by false order book momentum or toxic manipulation.

### Principle 3: Functional vs. Heuristic Attribution (The Deletion Gain Proof)

#### The Principle 3 Mechanism

Legacy explainability systems (such as linear regression coefficients or heuristic feature importance) simply measure the numerical magnitude or gradient slope of a variable.

Dubstrata's Holistic Relevance eXplanation (HRX) calculates **true functional dependency via Deletion Gain ($\Delta\text{Gain}$)**:
$$\Delta\text{Gain}(Q_k) = \mathcal{L}(X_{\setminus Q_k}) - \mathcal{L}(X)$$
where $\mathcal{L}$ is the forecast prediction error (symmetric quantile loss) and $X_{\setminus Q_k}$ represents the input tensor with quadrant $Q_k$ masked with neutral Gaussian pseudo-noise.

#### The Principle 3 Learning

Attribution weights reflect **informational indispensability, not feature magnitude**. When the Causal Graph weighting surges from $4.8\%$ to $16.5\%$, it means that *in that exact market state, stripping out knowledge graph topology increases forecast error by a severe penalty*. The quadrant weights tell the portfolio manager exactly which data substrate is keeping the model grounded.

### Principle 4: Epistemic Disagreement as an Endogenous Risk Gauge

#### The Principle 4 Mechanism

Large price moves often occur late in a market sequence. Dubstrata's 16 channels allow researchers to detect **cross-domain micro-divergences before they materialize in spot price action**:

1. **Microstructure Order Flow Imbalance (Ch 03 - OFI/VPIN):** Measures toxic aggressive market orders hitting the resting limit order book.
2. **Prediction Market Entropy Conviction (Ch 06 - $\Psi$):** Measures whether decentralized participants possess high consensus or chaotic disagreement:
   $$\Psi_{\text{CSI}} = \text{sign}(\Delta P) \cdot \frac{1 - H(p)}{H_{\max}} \cdot \ln(1 + \text{Vol})$$
3. **Friedkin-Johnsen Graph Belief Equilibrium (Ch 09 - $Y$):** Models the sociological belief shift across institutional entities in the knowledge graph:
   $$Y^{(t+1)} = \Lambda W Y^{(t)} + (I - \Lambda) Y^{(0)}$$

When raw spot price is barely moving (e.g., drifting down $\$16$ on an $\$83,000$ asset), but OFI surges, $\Psi$ collapses, and the Friedkin-Johnsen belief vector flips from positive to negative, the model recognizes **epistemic conflict**.

#### The Principle 4 Learning

**Cross-domain conflict compresses forecast certainty.** When the knowledge graph (Q3) and prediction markets (Q2) contradict the short-term order book (Q1), the model widens its quantile fan ($q_{10}$ to $q_{90}$) and suppresses directional leverage. It prevents the strategy from chasing transient order book noise.

### Quadrant 3: ArcadeDB Causal Knowledge Graph
